BTree Hunger Games

A server-wide survival event on Minecraft 1.21. 100 players. 4 sections. 3 lives each. One winner.

Apply Now View Roster

How It Works

  1. 1. Apply

    Fill out the application form with your Minecraft gamertag and email. Applications are reviewed by admins.

  2. 2. Pay on Patreon

    Once accepted, pay for your spot via our Patreon. You'll receive a receipt with a unique transaction code.

  3. 3. Submit Your Receipt

    Come back to this site and submit your Patreon receipt code along with a screenshot link for verification.

  4. 4. Get Whitelisted

    After admin verification you'll be added to the server whitelist and assigned a starting section.

  5. 5. Fight

    On event day — 4 sections, 3 lives, last one standing wins.

Key Rules

  • 3 lives per player
  • 4 sections: Red, Orange, Blue, Purple
  • No moving during spawn-lock (60 seconds)
  • Eliminated players are kicked from the server
  • Final survivor is teleported to the finale arena
  • No rejoin after elimination
